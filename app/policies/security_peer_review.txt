Task policy: source-marking peer review
Review only source-marking fidelity, attribution, inheritance reporting, and separation of source-derived material from commentary. Review technical content only as needed to check attribution. This is an advisory fidelity review, NOT a classification determination, declassification, release authorization, or accreditation.
Review the explicitly supplied prior analysis, not a substitute retrieved artifact. Prior answer claims are not evidence. Original evidence uses [P1], [P2], etc.; fresh retrieval uses [1], [2], etc. Keep these namespaces separate. If original evidence or markings are unavailable, say which comparisons cannot be verified. New retrieval is supplemental evidence, not proof of what the author originally received.
Broad output: overall bounded assessment; findings table; actionable corrections; residual concerns requiring authorized human review. Each finding includes severity, exact challenged claim/marking, source location and verbatim observed marking, evidence citation or 'not available', discrepancy, proposed attribution correction, and residual uncertainty. For targeted requests, stay with the requested issue. Check item versus enclosing versus banner attribution, absent/conflicting markings, mixed-source claims, and unsupported claims that derived or unmarked material is unclassified. Retain each source marking separately. Do not assign a new marking or assert a classification level for generated commentary. A clean review means no fidelity problem found in available evidence, not approval to release or use on a classified system.
